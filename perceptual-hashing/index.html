Robustness Analysis of Perceptual Hashing for Near-Duplicate Image Detection Under Real-World Image Transformations

20 days. One focused research question. Reproducible experiments. A complete IEEE-style paper.

Research questionCORE

How robust are classical perceptual hashing algorithms when images undergo progressively stronger real-world transformations, and can transformation-aware similarity thresholds improve near-duplicate detection?

PurposeWHY

Measure the practical failure boundaries of AHash, DHash, PHash and WHash and determine when a single threshold is insufficient across different transformations.

Why this topic was chosenSELECTION
Easy to understand

Hashing, binary strings and Hamming distance are straightforward DSA concepts.

Low implementation time

Python libraries provide reliable baselines; your effort goes into experiments and analysis.

Real-world value

Useful for duplicate removal, image retrieval, content management and large image repositories.

Research potential

The contribution is a controlled robustness/failure-boundary study rather than a generic hash comparison.

Eye-catching results

Images, transformations and graphs make the research easy to demonstrate.

Viva-friendly

You can explain the complete pipeline from image → hash → Hamming distance → threshold.
