# Source verification notes

The research direction was checked against current literature. Particularly important sources:

- Du, Ho, Cong (2020), *Perceptual hashing for image authentication: A survey* — ScienceDirect. The survey covers image-hashing methods, datasets and evaluation metrics.
- Biswas & Blanco-Medina (2021), *State of the Art: Image Hashing* — arXiv: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.11794
- Dolhansky & Canton Ferrer (2020), *Adversarial collision attacks on image hashing functions* — arXiv: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.09473
- Mckeown & Buchanan (2022), *Hamming Distributions of Popular Perceptual Hashing Techniques* — arXiv: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.08035
- Venkatesan et al. (2000), *Robust Image Hashing* — Microsoft Research.
- Tang et al. (2020), *Robust image hashing with compressed sensing and ordinal measures* — open access Springer.
- Singh & Farid (2019), *Robust Homomorphic Image Hashing* — CVPR Workshops open access.
- Jakhar & Borah (2025), *Effective near-duplicate image detection using perceptual hashing and deep learning*.
- *Comparative Evaluation of Perceptual Hashing and Deep Embedding Methods for Robust and Efficient Image Deduplication* (2026) — MDPI.

Important: a few older publisher papers have unstable direct-PDF URLs or require institutional access. The website therefore includes open repository/author links where available. Never use a pirated copy.

- Hany Farid (2021), *An Overview of Perceptual Hashing* — direct Semantic Scholar PDF: https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/7ac2/9cdaa62a16606e40d5f80b60cd583aaaba91.pdf
- Swaminathan, Mao & Wu (2006), *Robust and Secure Image Hashing* — direct University of Maryland PDF: https://terpconnect.umd.edu/~minwu/public_paper/Jnl/0606hash_IEEErev_TIFS.pdf
- Davarzani et al. (2015), *Image authentication using LBP-based perceptual image hashing* — direct CiteSeerX PDF: https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=f8a3572eee983cfe0ec21819123131e78d8a88b6&repid=rep1&type=pdf
